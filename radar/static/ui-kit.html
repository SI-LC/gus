Gus UI kit

Every cockpit screen is built from these parts. Use them as they are. If a screen needs something new, add it to radar/cockpit.css and to this page first.

The rules
  • One primary button per screen or card: the thing you most likely want to do. A button that sits on every screen is never primary.
  • Lime means the likely next step, and nothing else: not a count, a heading, a filter or a label.
  • Only buttons look like buttons. A label is text with a dot, never a box.
  • Something you can't take back is red.
  • A screen explains itself: no paragraph of instructions under the title.
  • A button says what happens ("Shortlist", "Skip"), never "OK" or "Submit".
  • Colour never carries meaning alone: a dot or an edge always comes with words.
  • Colours, fonts and corners come from the tokens below, never a new hex value.
  • Every list has an empty state that says what will appear there.

A line that opens

For any list that can grow long: a person to contact, a follow-up, a company. One line says what it is and, in bold, the reason to look. It opens in place for the rest. A move at a company whose product you can get into free says so on the line (.pill), and opened, its reasons start with the hook and a link to the product. Never a full card for every item: 360 of them is a page nobody reads.
